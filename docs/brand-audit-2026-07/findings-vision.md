1. The brand caught the category noun but not the full 2026-07-03 vision move.
2. Biggest gaps: per-viewer truth, sovereign export, and individual/free-tier breadth.
3. Messaging still overuses graph and compounding as mechanisms before outcomes.
4. Differentiators need re-anchoring to the amended moat, not generic transparency/privacy.
5. "Compounding intelligence" should stay, but only with a mechanism-backed explanation.
6. Retrieval-superiority restraint and no-LLM AI posture are currently aligned.
