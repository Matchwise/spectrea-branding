## Lens Verdict

Spectrea's brand system is strongest at philosophy, palette, logo basics, motion, and broad copy tone.
The main coverage failure is operationalization for AI and implementers.
Too many rules live in prose or TSX demos while `brand.ts` remains too sparse to generate complete references.
The most product-critical gap is still graph/data-viz rendering.
The most brand-risky verbal gaps are AI naming, trust/legal disclosure copy, and missing product-surface microcopy patterns.
The audience breadth is acknowledged in the live page but not propagated to canonical/LLM-readable surfaces.
Governance needs one current version, one owner map, and one source-of-truth hierarchy.
