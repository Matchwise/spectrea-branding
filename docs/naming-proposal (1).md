# MatchWise Rebrand: Balanced Naming Proposal

**Date:** 2 March 2026
**Status:** Working Draft — Validation ongoing
**Prepared for:** Darren, CEO, MatchWise

---

## Executive Summary

This proposal addresses a critical gap identified in the original naming sprint: **all 65 candidates were coined/fanciful words** — a single naming direction. The brand audit explicitly recommended exploring **4–5 naming directions** with 50–100 candidates total.

This document rebalances the portfolio across **four naming types**, with ~25 candidates per type, each accompanied by a brand rationale, validation status, and conflict assessment.

---

## Brand Criteria (from Audit)

Any name must satisfy:

- **Unique namespace** — not crowded by existing entities in search results
- **Strong domain** — .com, .ai, or .io obtainable
- **Trademark-registrable** — arbitrary or fanciful preferred (strongest protection classes)
- **No dating/matchmaking connotation** — critical lesson from "MatchWise"
- **Enterprise-appropriate** — suitable for financial advisory, professional services, manufacturing verticals
- **Evokes intelligence, knowledge, structure** — aligned with GraphRAG knowledge management platform

---

## Naming Types Framework

| Type | Description | Trademark Strength | Examples in Market |
|------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **A. Coined / Fanciful** | Pure inventions with no dictionary meaning | ★★★★★ Strongest | Xerox, Kodak, Asana |
| **B. Evocative Blend** | Meaningful morpheme combinations suggesting qualities | ★★★★ Very Strong | Spotify (spot+identify), Shopify (shop+simplify) |
| **C. Metaphorical / Abstract** | Real words repurposed from unrelated domains | ★★★★ Strong-Arbitrary | Slack, Notion, Figma, Canopy |
| **D. Classical Derivation** | Roots from Latin, Greek, or other classical languages | ★★★★ Very Strong | Veritas, Palantir, Airtable |

---

## Risk Ratings

- 🟢 **LOW** — No active conflicts found; likely registrable
- 🟡 **LOW-MEDIUM** — Minor conflicts in unrelated industries; defensible
- 🟠 **MEDIUM** — Some conflicts exist; requires legal review
- 🔴 **ELIMINATED** — Active conflicts in tech/SaaS/knowledge space
